Fishcraft 1.0.0 — World of Warcraft: Forever

Extract the Fishcraft folder into the Forever client's Interface/AddOns folder.
The final path must be Interface/AddOns/Fishcraft/Fishcraft.toc, without another
nested Fishcraft folder. Keep your character's SavedVariables when updating.
Restart the client after first installation and enable Fishcraft in AddOns.

/fc opens the radial menu. /fc settings opens settings. /fc diag prints addon
and client details. /fishcraft is an alias for /fc.
